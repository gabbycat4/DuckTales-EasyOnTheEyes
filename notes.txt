== Amazon route (tests/routes/full_amazon.txt, cycle host, console-faithful, 9,600 frames) ==
logs/amazon.compact (tools/measure/compact.py), logs/amazon_scroll*.txt (scroll_from_compact.py)
- Camera X = $F3:$F2, 512 wide. Steps: 1,2,1,2 (period 2) while walking (1.5 px/frame).
  Histogram: 0 x6119, +-1 x1835, +-2 x1636; cuts (warps, respawn) 18-167 px.
- Starts and stops are INSTANT: the first steps of a run are already 1,2,1 or 2,1,2; it stops
  dead the same way. (NG1 case -> camera easing is needed.)
- 84 one-frame pauses inside runs: lag frames ($92 ticks, Scrooge doesn't move), in the air /
  pogo / states 9 and 13. The native player's No slowdown (default) removes them.
- Vertical row change ($E9, 61 frames): ~30 frames with the playfield still (the game draws the
  new screen), then 29 frames moving 8 px a frame (one tile row) = a whole screen. Fast:
  MM2's ladder rules (hidden edge rows, fast-scenery easing) apply. 7 of them in the Amazon.
- $E9 is also set for 2-4 frames, every 8 frames, in row 1 from frame ~6959 (boss area?):
  a screen shake? To look at.
- Scrooge is kept at screen X 128 while the camera can move, so his sprites stand still on
  screen during walks; the background does the moving. A trailing (eased) camera must move
  him too (world X - eased camera).
- OAM: 212 of 9,599 frames reorder the same sprites (some rotation); the port's No sprite
  flicker compositor sets the order anyway.
